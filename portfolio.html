AURA

2026 · Major project, team of 2

An AI companion that lives across AR glasses, a smartwatch and a desk robot, and reacts to how you feel.

  • Designed every part in Fusion 360 and printed them in PETG and PLA+, including a glasses frame small enough to hold a custom PCB.
  • Laid out that PCB in EasyEDA and had it made by JLCPCB.
  • Wrote the ESP32-S3 firmware in C++ for the glasses and the watch, with LVGL animations for the AI's 7 emotions.
  • Built the Flutter app that syncs all three devices over Bluetooth and does the heavy processing.
  • The robot reads your mood from its camera using Python and MediaPipe. Heart rate, SpO2 and GPS also shape what the AI says.

HAND

2025 · Mini project, solo

A smart glove for hospital patients who can't easily speak or move: 25 gestures, live vitals, and a headset built into the fingers.

  • Tracks heart rate and SpO2 continuously with a MAX30102 on an ESP32-S3 Mini, with firmware in C++.
  • Five flex sensors, one per finger, combine into 25 gestures: call a nurse, ask for water or food, or switch the room's light, fan and TV over IR.
  • A Kotlin app watches every glove on the ward over one shared Wi-Fi network.
  • Works as a Bluetooth headset too. Speakers sit in two fingertips and a mic in the palm, so holding your hand to your face works like a phone.
